## 1. Platform Overview

**AuraCast** is an enterprise-grade social media marketing operating system powered by Gemini AI. It automates every step of the content creation flywheel:
- **Strategy**: AI analyzes your brand niche and audience to craft 30-day growth blueprints.
- **Creation**: Generates platform-optimized text copy, visual graphic cards, and short-form video storyboards.
- **Publishing**: Queues and dispatches content across Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), Facebook, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn.
- **Engagement**: Centralizes messages and generates sentiment-aware AI replies.
- **Intelligence**: Tracks performance metrics, competitor benchmarks, and revenue conversions.

## 2. Navigation & Workspace Modules

AuraCast provides an intuitive top navigation header for seamless switching between specialized hubs:


| :--- | :--- |

| **Team & Settings** | Manage team invitations, editorial approval workflows, brand guidelines (Brand Kit), and workspace billing. |

## 3. User Guide & Workflows

### Module 1: Dashboard & Onboarding
The **Dashboard** serves as your launchpad:
1. **Hero Banner**: Displays an overview of your audience reach and key marketing velocity metrics.
2. **How AuraCast Works (4 Steps)**:
   - **Step 1: Generate AI Strategy**: Create multi-channel growth playbooks.
   - **Step 2: Auto-Create Content**: Generate captions, graphics, and video scripts.
   - **Step 3: Schedule & Publish**: Queue posts into the rolling calendar.
   - **Step 4: Analyze & Scale**: Monitor engagement trends and revenue conversions.
3. **10 OS Modules Grid**: Shortcuts to every specialized tool in the workspace.

---

### Module 2: AI Strategy & Campaign Engine
Located under **AI Strategy**:
1. **Generating a 30-Day Blueprint**:
   - Enter your brand positioning (e.g., *"Christian Motivational & Growth Page for Young Nigerians"*).
   - Select your target post frequency (3 to 7 days per week) and primary goals (Brand Awareness, Community Engagement, Lead Generation).
   - Click **Generate Strategy Roadmap**.
2. **"Spark Post" Instant Trigger**:
   - Each weekly post concept in your blueprint includes a green **⚡ Spark Post** button.
   - Clicking **Spark Post** instantly transfers the topic directly into the **Content Studio**, where AI automatically drafts captions, graphic design layouts, and hashtag packages.
3. **Autonomous Campaign Builder**:
   - Create multi-week themed campaigns (e.g., *"Product Launch Blitz"*, *"30-Day Leadership Challenge"*).
   - Review AI-generated hooks and approve campaign milestones before queueing.

---

### Module 3: Content Studio & Visual Creator
Located under **Content Studio**:
1. **Topic Input & AI Generation**:
   - Enter any daily topic or concept in the Topic Creator.
   - Select target channels (Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp).
   - Click **Generate Branded Package**.
2. **Graphic Design Card Customization**:
   - Customize background colors, gradient overlays, font pairings, visual quote text, and logo placement.
   - Click **Generate AI Background Image** to create custom visual assets via Gemini.
3. **Caption Composer**:
   - View channel-specific captions tailored to platform character limits and hashtag best practices.
   - Edit, copy, or refine copy directly in the editor.

---

### Module 4: AI Video Studio
Located within the **Content Studio** page:
1. **Generating Video Storyboards**:
   - Input a video concept or select an existing post topic.
   - AI drafts scene-by-scene visual descriptions, text overlays, and voiceover scripts.
2. **Audio & Narration Preview**:
   - Synthesize or preview voiceover audio loops.
   - Save completed video projects directly to the **Content Calendar**.

---

### Module 5: Content Calendar & Scheduler
Located under **Calendar**:
1. **Rolling 7-Day Scheduler**:
   - View queued and published posts mapped chronologically across the upcoming week.
2. **Rescheduling & Status Updates**:
   - Update dispatch times or switch post status between `Draft`, `Scheduled`, and `Published`.
3. **Instant Batch Dispatch**:
   - Click **Publish Now** on any scheduled card to broadcast content immediately to connected social channels.

---

### Module 6: Unified Social Inbox
Located under **Social Inbox**:
1. **Cross-Platform Message Stream**:
   - Monitor incoming messages and comments from Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, and LinkedIn in one feed.
2. **AI Smart Reply**:
   - Click **Suggest AI Reply** on any message. The AI analyzes the user's intent and drafts a warm, brand-aligned response.
3. **Sentiment Tagging**:
   - Messages are automatically tagged as **Positive**, **Inquisitive**, or **Feedback** to prioritize urgent customer inquiries.

---

### Module 7: Analytics & Performance Intelligence
Located under **Analytics**:
1. **Recharts Multi-Platform Area Chart**:
   - Interactive 7-day visualization tracking engagement across Twitter, Instagram, and Facebook.
2. **CSV Data Export**:
   - Click **Export Analytics CSV** to download raw performance metrics for offline reporting.
3. **Competitor Intelligence**:
   - Benchmark your brand against industry competitors on reach, posting frequency, and engagement score.
4. **Lead & Conversion Tracker**:
   - Track click-through rates, lead form submissions, and direct revenue attribution (e.g., ₦2.5M revenue tracked).

---

### Module 8: Autonomous A/B Testing Studio
Located under **AI Strategy**:
1. **Setting Up Experiments**:
   - Define Variant A (Control) and Variant B (Challenger) with different hooks, emojis, or calls-to-action.
2. **Automated Winner Selection**:
   - AuraCast monitors early engagement velocity and automatically promotes the winning variant to the main calendar.

---

### Module 9: Brand Kit & Team Collaboration
Located under **Team & Settings**:
1. **Brand Kit Management**:
   - Define primary brand colors, typography fonts, tone of voice guidelines, target persona keywords, and brand logo URL.
   - All AI content generation automatically adheres to your saved Brand Kit rules.
2. **Team Roles & Permissions**:
   - Invite team members with specific access levels: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**.
   - Enable **Editorial Approval Workflows** requiring admin sign-off before content is scheduled or published.

---

### Module 10: Developer API & Security Vault
Located under **Team & Settings** -> **Developer API**:
1. **API Key Management**: Generate secure API keys for programmatic access.
2. **Webhooks & Integrations**: Configure webhooks for real-time post dispatch events.
3. **Enterprise Security**: View system audit logs, compliance checks, and encryption status.

## 4. Aura Copilot (AI Assistant)

**Aura Copilot** is your floating AI marketing assistant:
- Click the **Ask Aura Copilot** button in the top navigation or bottom launcher bar.
- Ask questions like:
  - *"How do I write a high-converting caption for a fitness page?"*
  - *"Create 5 viral video hooks for a real estate brand."*
  - *"Explain how the Spark Post feature works."*
- Click **Apply to Workspace** on any generated response to automatically load topics or captions directly into your creative workspace.

## 5. Data Architecture & Security

- **Database**: AuraCast utilizes **Firebase Firestore** for cloud persistence, storing brand blueprints, topics, calendar items, team members, and analytics logs.
- **Authentication**: Built-in **Firebase Authentication** supporting secure user sign-in and session management.
- **Server-Side API Shielding**: All Gemini AI API keys, third-party credentials, and Meta tokens remain securely on the Express server (`server.ts`) and are never exposed to the client browser.

## 6. Troubleshooting & FAQs

### Q: What happens when I click "Spark Post" in the AI Strategy blueprint?
**A**: Clicking **Spark Post** takes the selected post concept, automatically navigates you to the **Content Studio**, and populates the topic creator with tailored captions, graphic card layouts, and target channel selections ready for immediate editing or scheduling.

### Q: Can I export my analytics reports?
**A**: Yes! Go to the **Analytics & Intelligence** tab and click **Export Analytics CSV** to download a formatted CSV file containing 7-day multi-platform comment and engagement data.

### Q: How do I invite team members to my workspace?
**A**: Navigate to **Team & Settings** -> **Team Collaboration**, enter your teammate's email address, select their role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer), and click **Send Invite**.
